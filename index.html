Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Mega Banana operates Pomodoro Anywhere. You may use the timer without signing in. When you sign in, connect Google Calendar, or enable notifications, additional information is processed to provide those features.

Information we process

  • Timer settings, categories, session history, and device-generated identifiers are stored on your device. If you sign in, this information is sent to our service to synchronize it across your devices.
  • Google sign-in provides your name, email address, account identifier, and, when available, profile image URL. We use these to identify your account and provide synchronization.
  • If you connect Google Calendar, the app requests access to list calendars and manage calendars and events. We use that access to let you choose or create a calendar and export your completed or saved focus sessions. The service stores an encrypted credential for this connection.
  • If you enable completion notifications, a device registration token and related delivery information are processed to send them.
  • When you contact support, we receive the information you include in your message.

Service providers

Cloudflare hosts the synchronization service. Google provides sign-in, Calendar and Firebase messaging. Apple provides platform services including notifications. These providers process information as needed to provide their services.

Your choices and deletion

You can use the timer without an account, disconnect Calendar, or turn off notifications. You can delete your account in the app. Account deletion removes the server account data, synchronization records, sessions, device registrations and stored Calendar credentials; revocation of the Google grant is attempted. You can also manage connected access in your Google account. Data stored locally on a device may need to be removed on that device.

Security and retention

We use HTTPS for network communication and encrypt stored Calendar credentials. We retain account and synchronized data while needed to provide the service or until account deletion, subject to applicable obligations and operational records maintained by service providers.
